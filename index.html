callmejo

I’m Joyal Johnson, Jo for short. My journey started in Kerala and brought me to Paisley, Scotland, and my camera has been with me the whole way.

I’m drawn to light: the way autumn sets the trees on fire, how neon turns a fairground into something cinematic, how a single beam of sun can make an ordinary moment feel like a memory. At events, I’m the one in the crowd catching the laugh mid-dance rather than the stiff group pose (though I’ll get that too).

This site is a small, hand-picked selection of my work, not everything I shoot. For the day-to-day, you’ll find me on Instagram at @ig.callmejo.

Outside photography I work in digital marketing, so I understand what a good image has to do for a brand, a society or an event, as well as how it looks.

Don’t let your passion fade away. This is how I keep mine alive.
